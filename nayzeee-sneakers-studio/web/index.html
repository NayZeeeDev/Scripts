GUIDE
From clothing to props

1Scan

  • Pick your server's resources folder and press Scan for shoes.
  • It finds every shoe in your addon clothing packs: files named like mp_m_freemode_01_mypack^feet_007_u.ydd with their feet_diff_..._a_uni.ytd colourways.
  • Plain downloads (feet_000_u.ydd) show up too. They convert fine, but you link them to their drawable in game.

2Pick and name

  • Click a card to select it. New and Changed shoes are selected for you.
  • Give each shoe a name. Names can be changed in game later too.
  • The box size is picked from the shoe's shape: sneakers go upright in a shoe box, heels and tall boots lie on their side in a heel or boot box. Change it if you like.

3Convert

  • Each colourway becomes its own prop with the texture built in, packed to sit in its box, with collision.
  • Skin on open-toe heels is cut away, heavy meshes are reduced, and two inventory icons are made per colourway (loose and boxed).
  • With Copy icons to the inventory on, the icons go straight into ox_inventory / qb-inventory.

4On the server

  • Put nayzeee-sneakers-props in your resources and start it before the script:
    ensure nayzeee-sneakers-props
    ensure nayzeee-sneakers
  • Restart, then type /sneakerstudio in game to price the new shoes and switch them on.

5Later

  • Added or removed clothing packs? Scan again. Only new and changed shoes are converted, and shoes that are gone are removed.
  • The in-game studio spots new and removed drawables by itself, so it also tells you when it's time to run this again.
